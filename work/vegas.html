Meow Wolf: Las Vegas

Custom 3D AR for Omega Mart on the Las Vegas Strip

Overflow was a series of ten AR experiences for Meow Wolf across Las Vegas and New Mexico. On the Las Vegas Strip it put custom 3D pieces in the street, including giant Lemon Frenzys, Orange Mitosis, and a T-Bone Drone Show. The campaign was a Webby finalist.

It was built entirely for the web, with high-fidelity volumetric capture and Gaussian splat environments, so people could open it from the street with no app required. I was the lead engineer and designed the architecture.
