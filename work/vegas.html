Meow Wolf: Las Vegas

Portals to Omega Mart on the Las Vegas Strip

Overflow was a series of ten AR experiences for Meow Wolf across Las Vegas and New Mexico, including portals to Omega Mart from the Las Vegas Strip. The campaign was a Webby finalist.

It used web-based volumetric capture and Gaussian splats to open portals into Meow Wolf worlds from the street, with no app required. I led the AR work.
