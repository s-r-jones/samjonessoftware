Meow Wolf: Las Vegas

Custom 3D AR for Omega Mart on the Las Vegas Strip

Overflow was ten AR experiences for Meow Wolf across Las Vegas and New Mexico, and it was a Webby finalist. On the Las Vegas Strip we put custom 3D pieces right in the street: giant Lemon Frenzys, Orange Mitosis, and a T-Bone Drone Show.

All of it was built for the web with custom rendering and 3D animation, so anyone could open it from the street with no app. I was the lead engineer and designed the architecture.
