Iris

On-device machine learning for surgical sterile processing

IRIS helps hospital sterile processing teams get surgical trays right. It uses computer vision that runs on the device to recognize each instrument, walks technicians through sterilization, and lets them verify a tray before it goes back to the OR.

I was the lead engineer and tech lead. I designed the architecture and led the team that built all of it, from the on-device models to the app technicians use at their workstation.

It started in the hospital. We spent a lot of hours watching surgery, then shadowed sterile processing leaders and technicians. Prototype sessions with them shaped how the app works, how the station is set up, and what the product needed to do.

Sterile processing is fast, crowded, and usually a hot, humid basement with bad wifi. People are in PPE or gloves, handling wet instruments, and moving between stations. So we designed around where the device sits, the camera angle, and how quickly it responds.
