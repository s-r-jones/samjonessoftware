About

I'm a full-stack design engineer with more than a decade of experience taking ideas and turning them into real products. I co-founded Refract Studio in Santa Fe, where I shape the concept, design the architecture, and build it, from distributed backends to on-device computer vision to multiplayer XR.

My products run in hard places: hospital basements with limited wifi, phones on a city street, a room full of strangers sharing one virtual space. I've built them for Google, Snap, IKEA, Meow Wolf, and the cities of Albuquerque and Santa Fe, and the work has won MIT Reality Hack and MIT Founders Lab and been nominated for Webby and Auggie awards.

Projects

Iris

Meow Wolf: Las Vegas

Custom 3D AR on the Las Vegas Strip for Meow Wolf's Omega Mart, from giant Lemon Frenzys to a T-Bone Drone Show, in the browser with no app download. I was lead engineer. Webby finalist, 2025.

Route 66 Remixed

An 18-mile public art experience on Albuquerque's Central Avenue for the Route 66 Centennial, with browser-based AR by more than 20 New Mexico artists. I was lead engineer and tech lead.

Ojos Diferentes

Ready Care

Yeigo

Doulie

IKEA Biocompanion

An AR concept for SPACE10, IKEA's innovation lab: a small digital bird lands in your palm and shares air quality the way a friend would. I was lead engineer.

Meow Wolf: New Mexico

Obsidiopolis
