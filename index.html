About

I build software. I build it to hold.

I co-founded Refract Studio with Lauren Cason out here in the high desert of Santa Fe. We make things for hospitals, museums, city streets, and the strange places in between. I design the architecture for all of it. Distributed systems that stay up when the crowd shows. Computer vision that runs on the device, in a hot basement, with no wifi. Multiplayer XR where a room full of strangers shares the same impossible space.

For ten years the work has come from Google, Snap, IKEA, Meow Wolf, and the cities of Albuquerque and Santa Fe. Some of it won: MIT Reality Hack, MIT Founders Lab. Some of it was up for Webbys and Auggies. The work is the thing.

Highlights

  • Meow Wolf ticketing. Everyone who wanted into Meow Wolf came through one web page. I led the rebuild of tickets.meowwolf.com from the first plan to launch. It added $2M a year. Conversion rose 5%. Checkout lost 40 seconds, and nobody missed them.
  • IRIS. We went into the hospital first, into the surgeries and the sterile processing basements. Then we built a computer-vision app that knows every instrument on sight and checks every tray before it goes back to the OR.
  • Omega Mart AR. We were somewhere on the Las Vegas Strip when the portals began to take hold. Meow Wolf's web AR campaign. Webby finalist.
